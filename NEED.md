# The need

## Problem
I have roadmaps for many things (cyber, AI, Python, GSoC, calisthenics) in separate Notion pages. I have low consistency, I get distracted, and having many roadmaps means I spend energy choosing instead of doing.

## What fails with normal tools
- Long task lists feel heavy, so I open nothing.
- Streaks and overdue lists make a missed day feel like failure, so I quit.
- Notes and plans live in one place and my phone, where I get distracted, lives in another.

## What I need
1. One roadmap that merges every track.
2. One next action at a time, small enough to start now.
3. Forgiving tracking: weekly goals, not daily streaks.
4. Reminders and a buddy that nudges me, on the phone.
5. Proof of work that builds up in public (projects first, learn in public).

## Who it is for
Me first. Anyone who wants to do many things but loses momentum.

## How I will know it works
I open it on most days and finish at least 4 sessions a week for 4 weeks in a row.
